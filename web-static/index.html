How payouts work

  • Rewards go straight to your address in each block the pool finds. There is no balance to withdraw and no minimum payout.
  • Your share of a block is proportional to your work in the recent shares (PPLNS): up to 3 blocks' worth of pool work, and never more than 3 days.
  • Coinbase coins can be spent after 100 confirmations (about 17 hours).
  • Node fee: –. Fixed share difficulty: add /DIFF to the username, e.g. ecash:qq…/500000.
